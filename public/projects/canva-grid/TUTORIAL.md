# Tutorial

In-app walkthrough shown on first visit. Access anytime via the menu.

## Step 1: Welcome

CanvaGrid helps you create visual designs — social posts, ads, presentations, stories, and more. Your design is made up of cells — the boxes in your grid. Each cell can hold an image, text, or both.

**Suggested workflow:** Pick a preset, add images, write text, tweak the layout, style it, download.

## Step 2: Presets

Start here to quickly set up your design.

- **Platform:** Pick a size for your design — Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, print, and more. This sets the canvas dimensions. Use the search box to find platforms by name.
- **Layout:** Choose how to arrange your cells — single image, side-by-side, stacked, and more. Filter by shape (square, tall, wide).
- **Themes:** Pick a color scheme from 19 presets, each with light and dark variants. Or set your own custom colors.
- **Looks:** Apply a visual style — changes fonts and image effects without touching your layout or colors.

Presets are just a starting point — you can customize everything after.

## Step 3: Media

Add images to your design.

- **Sample Images:** Browse free sample images by category — click one to add it.
- **Images:** Upload your own images, then assign them to cells. Adjust fit and position per image.
- **Image Overlay & Filters:** Add a color tint to any image, or apply filters like black & white, sepia, or blur.
- **Logo:** Optionally add a logo and choose its position and size.

Check out the AI Prompt Helper for ideas to use with AI image generators.

## Step 4: Content

Add text to your design. There are two modes for entering text — edit directly in the cell, or use the content panel for bulk editing.

## Step 5: Structure

Control the grid structure — number of rows and columns, cell spacing, padding, and aspect ratio.

## Step 6: Style

Fine-tune the visual appearance — fonts, text sizes, colors, borders, shadows, and cell overlays.

## Step 7: Export

Download your design as a PNG image, or use the multi-page export for documents with multiple pages.

## Step 8: Tips

- Use keyboard shortcuts for faster editing
- Try different looks to quickly explore visual styles
- Multi-page mode works great for slide decks and stories
