# Tutorial

In-app walkthrough shown on first visit. Access anytime via the menu.

## Step 1: Welcome to FuelHunt

Find the cheapest fuel near you in South Africa. We show you stations sorted by price and distance so you always get the best deal.

## Step 2: Search for stations

Type an address or area name to find stations nearby. Or tap the crosshair button to use your current GPS location.

## Step 3: Filter your results

Choose the fuel type you need (like ULP 95 or Diesel) and set how far you want to search. Tap "Reset" to clear all filters.

## Step 4: View results

Stations appear on the map and in the list below. Pull the list up to browse more stations. They're sorted by cheapest price first — tap "Nearest" to sort by distance instead.

## Step 5: Station details

See all fuel prices with trend arrows showing if prices went up or down. View a 30-day price history chart and tap "Get Directions" to navigate there.

## Step 6: Menu & settings

Tap the menu icon in the top corner to switch between dark and light mode, access admin features, or check for app updates.
