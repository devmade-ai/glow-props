# Tutorial

In-app walkthrough shown on first visit. Access anytime via the menu.

## Step 1: Welcome to Graphiki

A visual workspace for building and exploring networks of ideas, people, companies, and concepts. Everything runs in your browser — no server, no account, no data leaves your device.

## Step 2: Create and Connect

Create items and add labels like "Person" or "Project" to categorize them. Right-click (or long-press) any item and choose "Connect to..." to link it to another item. Select multiple items with Ctrl+click to edit labels or properties in bulk.

## Step 3: Graph and Timeline

Switch between two views. Graph view shows your items as an interactive network — drag, zoom, and pan to explore. Timeline view orders items by any date property on a vertical timeline.

## Step 4: Search and Filter

Use the search bar to find items by label, property, or title. Try "label:Person" or "label:Company". Combine terms with AND, OR, and NOT — for example, "label:Person OR label:Company".

## Step 5: Import and Export

Import data from JSON files with optional smart matching that detects near-duplicate items. Export your full graph as JSON for backup or sharing.

## Step 6: Analyze Your Data

Run graph analysis algorithms — degree centrality, betweenness, PageRank, connected components, shortest path, and clustering coefficient. Results are visualized directly on the graph.

## Step 7: Works Offline

Graphiki is a Progressive Web App. Install it on any device and use it without an internet connection. Your data is stored locally in the browser.
