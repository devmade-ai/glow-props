# Tutorial

A short walkthrough shown on first launch. Reachable anytime from the You tab → Help & tutorial. Four slides, focused on what makes inTXT different.

## Slide 1: Anonymous by design

No accounts. No names. No numbers. Your identity is just a random ID on your device — start chatting straight away, with nothing to sign up for.

## Slide 2: Tag what you mean

The signature feature: add up to 2 intention tags to a message — sincere, joking, serious, or your own word — in color, either while composing or after sending. The tag shows right on the message, so your meaning is never misread. Always optional.

## Slide 3: On your terms

Send when it lands best — schedule a message up to 30 days out. Edit within 15 minutes, reply, react with emoji, and see ✓ / ✓✓ read receipts. The familiar things, done simply.

## Slide 4: Private by default

Your business stays yours. Notifications are generic unless you opt in to message previews. Uninstall and you're gone — no recovery, no backdoors, no account resets. You are your device.
