# Tutorial

In-app walkthrough shown on first visit. Access anytime from Settings.

## Step 1: Welcome to SyncTone

Anonymous messaging where you tag the tone of your messages. No accounts. No phone numbers. Just conversations.

## Step 2: Creating a Chat

Tap "Create Chat" to get a unique code like "happy-tiger". Share the link or copy the code to send to someone. When they join, you're connected. Optionally name your chat (e.g., "Work Chat"). Only you see this name.

## Step 3: Joining a Chat

Got a code or link? Tap "Join Chat" and enter the code, or click a shared link to join automatically. You'll connect instantly. Each code works once.

## Step 4: Tone Tags

After sending a message, you can tag it with up to 2 tones. Pick from 6 defaults (happy, sad, serious, joking, sarcastic, sincere) or type your own. Tones are revealed when the other person opens the chat. This helps them understand your intent. Completely optional — use when helpful.

## Step 5: What's Familiar

React with emojis. Reply to messages. Edit messages (15 min). Delete messages. Copy text. See read receipts.

## Step 6: What's Different

No usernames or profiles. Tone tags (optional). Chat codes instead of contacts. You name your own chats. Messages auto-marked read.

## Step 7: Your Privacy

Your identity is a random ID on your device. Uninstall = gone forever. No recovery. No backdoors. No account resets. You are your device.

## Step 8: Stay Connected

Enable notifications to know when someone replies. Settings menu (gear icon, top-right) — mobile apps: one button, web browsers: two buttons. Enable anytime or skip.

## Step 9: You're Ready

That's it. Create or join a chat. Try tone tags. Communicate with clarity.
